## The reduced menu is the honest one

After midnight, a hotel cannot hide behind abundance. The menu becomes a statement of competence: soup, club sandwich, salad, pasta, tea, something sweet. If those basics are excellent, the kitchen has discipline.

## How to order well

Order food that survives the elevator. Fries rarely do. Broth, toast, rice, roast chicken, fruit, and a proper pot of tea are safer bets. Ask for the tray cleared in the morning unless you know you will be awake; the small mercy is part of the service.

## Why it matters

Late arrivals are when luxury stops being decorative. A hotel that can feed you quietly, accurately, and without making the room smell like regret understands care at the exact hour it is needed.
