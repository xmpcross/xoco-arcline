## Pack for transitions

A polished weekend has more thresholds than a casual one: lobby, pool, dinner, breakfast room, transfer, late checkout. The bag works when each transition is solved without repacking the room. Shoes, layers, and a small evening bag matter more than a third outfit.

## The room changes the list

If the hotel has a real steamer, skip half the armor. If dinner is formal, pack one piece that behaves under low light. If the pool is the point, bring swimwear that can dry by morning. Luxury travel punishes the almost-right item because every space is specific.

## What stays out

Do not bring backups for imagined lives. Bring a charger long enough for an inconvenient socket, one scent, sleepwear you can answer the door in, and sunglasses that can survive lunch. The rest is optimism with weight.
