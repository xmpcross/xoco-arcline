## The tray reveals the operation

Breakfast is where a hotel exposes its standards. Coffee arrives either hot or apologetic. Pastry is either crisp or steamed under a cloche. Fruit is either cut when ordered or tired from a refrigerator. None of these details are grand, which is why they matter.

## Order less, order better

The luxury move is restraint: coffee, juice, eggs or yogurt, one bread basket, one seasonal plate. A balcony table that can still hold a notebook and sunglasses feels more indulgent than a crowded cart that turns the morning into logistics.

## What to notice

Notice whether the server sets the table for the sun, whether salt and cutlery are complete, whether there is a quiet way to have the tray collected. A hotel that understands balcony breakfast understands that the room is not only for sleeping.
