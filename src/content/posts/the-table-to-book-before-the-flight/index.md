## Dinner as orientation

A good first dinner tells you what the city values: formality or looseness, local wine or global labels, late hours or early precision. It turns the place from a map into a mood. That is why the first table deserves more thought than the last.

## Choose the right kind of famous

Avoid the restaurant that exists only for visitors unless that is the point of the trip. Look for places with a strong bar, a confident local clientele, and enough polish to absorb a tired arrival. You want memorable, not demanding.

## Book around energy

If the flight lands late, book near the hotel. If you arrive mid-afternoon, book the room with the longer meal. If you are celebrating, tell them clearly. Restaurants can be generous with context, but they cannot read minds from a reservation name.
