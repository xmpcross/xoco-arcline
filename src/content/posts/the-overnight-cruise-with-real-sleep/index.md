## The cabin is the itinerary

Overnight travel works only when sleep is protected. Book away from elevators, entertainment rooms, and engine vibration if the vessel layout allows it. A balcony is lovely, but quiet is the upgrade that changes the next day.

## Dinner should not steal the night

The elegant mistake is booking the latest seating because it sounds festive. Eat early enough to walk the deck afterward and still sleep before the ship moves into its noisier hours. On water, pacing is part of comfort.

## Arrival is the reward

The point is waking already somewhere else, with luggage still folded and the day intact. A good overnight cruise handles breakfast, disembarkation, and transfers as one movement. If dawn feels like a queue, the magic has leaked out.
