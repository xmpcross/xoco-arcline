## The lounge has one job

A premium lounge should reduce the damage of travel. If it offers a reliable shower, quiet seating, real food, and boarding help, it can be worth an awkward fare difference. If it offers crowded chairs and sparkling wine at noon, it is simply another room at the airport.

## Measure against the layover

Under ninety minutes, skip it unless you need a shower. Between two and four hours, it can pay for itself in food, calm, and a reset. Overnight or very early connections are where lounge quality matters most, because one bad airport meal can follow you into the next day.

## The detail to check

Look for arrival access, not only departure access. Some of the most valuable lounge time happens after landing, before the room is ready. That is the hour when a shower and coffee feel less like luxury than rescue.
