## Soft names, hard questions

A spa menu is designed to calm you before it informs you. Ignore the adjectives first. Look for treatment length, what is included before and after, whether facilities are reserved or shared, and whether the therapist can adapt pressure, products, or pacing.

## The timing matters

Do not place the best treatment between checkout and a transfer. Book it on the first full day, late afternoon, when it can reset the trip instead of filling a gap. Leave an hour afterward for pool, tea, or doing absolutely nothing. That hour is part of the treatment even when it is not priced.

## The useful question

Ask what guests rebook. The answer will often be plainer than the signature ritual, and better. Hotels know which treatments survive repeat visitors and which were written for the brochure.
