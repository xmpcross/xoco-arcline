## Write the useful version

The useful email is short, specific, and early. Give dates, party size, dietary needs, mobility limits, celebration notes, and the tone you want: quiet, scene-y, local, formal, child-friendly, no late nights. Concierge teams are excellent at matching a brief when the brief is real.

## Ask for options, not miracles

Instead of asking for the best restaurant, ask for two dinner holds within a ten-minute taxi ride, one polished and one relaxed. Instead of asking what to do, ask for a private guide for architecture or galleries on the morning after arrival. The more defined the container, the better the result.

## The signal to watch

A strong reply explains trade-offs. It will say the famous table is available too late, the prettier beach has rougher water, or the closer driver is not the best driver. Luxury service is not agreement. It is judgment.
