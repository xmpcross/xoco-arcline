## The public room tells on itself

Before the suite, before the restaurant, before the spa, there is the lobby. It shows whether the hotel understands arrival: where bags go, whether check-in feels exposed, whether seating invites lingering or merely fills space.

## What to watch

Watch how staff handle people who are not checking in: guests waiting for cars, locals meeting for drinks, someone asking a small question. A luxury lobby is graceful under ordinary pressure. It has enough life to feel civic and enough control to feel calm.

## The best signal

The best lobbies give you a place to return to without making you perform. They hold flowers, light, chairs, and staff in balance. You know within ten minutes whether the hotel wants to impress you or host you.
