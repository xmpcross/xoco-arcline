## Land is the luxury

Golf resorts have something city hotels cannot manufacture: distance between buildings, maintained grounds, open horizon, and restaurants that understand daylight. Even if you never play, you benefit from the space the course protects.

## What non-golfers need

Check whether walking paths are open, whether the spa and pool are strong enough to carry a day, and whether restaurants avoid feeling like clubhouses after sunset. The resort should welcome a guest whose main sport is breakfast outside.

## The sweet spot

Coastal golf hotels are strongest in shoulder season, when the weather is mild and the room rates soften. Book a view over the course, not necessarily the ocean. Watching the light move across grass can be the quieter privilege.
